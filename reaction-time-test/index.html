How to use this test

  1. Start when ready

    Choose Start and keep your attention on the target.

  2. Wait for the change

    Do not click before the target changes.

  3. Click once

    Click as soon as it changes, then compare the result with another attempt.

What we check

The test records the interval between a browser-driven visual change and your click using a high-resolution browser timer.

A result reflects this page, your device, your input method and your attention during that attempt.

What this test can’t tell you

It is not a diagnosis, a measure of driving fitness, or a comparison against a population average.

Something not working?

I clicked too soon

Start a new attempt and wait for the colour change.

My result varies

That is expected. Try a few relaxed attempts rather than drawing a conclusion from one.

Common questions

Is this a medical test?

No. It is a simple browser timing exercise.

Does it save results?

No. Results stay on the page until you reset or leave.
