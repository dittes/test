TEST INSTITUTE / about

Small tests for everyday things.

Technology should leave you with fewer questions. We’re here to help with a few of them.

A check before the important moment

Before a call, after connecting a new keyboard, or when one headphone goes quiet, a quick check can help you decide what to do. Test Institute brings those checks together in a simple, familiar place.

The tool and the explanation

Each page pairs a working browser tool with instructions, a clear method, and practical troubleshooting. You can start immediately or read into the details. There is no account to create and no software to install.

Why “Institute”?

For us, it means taking small questions seriously: explaining our methods, stating our limits, and avoiding claims our tools cannot support. It is a name, not a claim of accreditation.
